Label

Wrap a control in a label and the spacing, hint text and choice layouts follow. The label is the layout primitive for every form control in this framework.

ClassDoes what
labelBlock label with spacing for the control it wraps
blockOn a label wrapping a radio: renders it as a selectable card

Label and control

<label class="label">
  Email
  <input type="email" class="input" placeholder="you@example.com" />
</label>
<label>
  Email
  <input type="email" placeholder="you@example.com" />
</label>

Hint text

A small after the control becomes a hint, and the control's bottom margin collapses into it.

<label class="label">
  Username
  <input type="text" class="input" />
  <small>Letters, numbers and dashes.</small>
</label>
<label>
  Username
  <input type="text" />
  <small>Letters, numbers and dashes.</small>
</label>

Invalid state

A label's leading p turns error-colored when the control after it is invalid.

<label class="label">
  <p>Email</p>
  <input type="email" class="input input-error" aria-invalid="true" value="not-an-email" />
  <small class="text-error">Enter a valid email address.</small>
</label>
<label>
  <p>Email</p>
  <input type="email" aria-invalid="true" value="not-an-email" />
  <small class="error">Enter a valid email address.</small>
</label>

Classless: <code>aria-invalid="true"</code> drives the error color on its own. <code>.input-error</code> is there for when you cannot set the attribute.
