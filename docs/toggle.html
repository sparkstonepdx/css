Toggle

A switch. .toggle goes on a checkbox; Kobalte's Switch builds it from a Control and a Thumb, with the real input visually hidden. Both shapes work.

alpha The class names and states here are stable. The look is not: these styles can change in a minor release, without a major version bump.

ClassDoes what
toggleThe track, on a checkbox or on Kobalte's Control
toggle-thumbThe knob, on Kobalte's Thumb. Not needed on a checkbox

Basic

<label class="label">
  <input type="checkbox" class="toggle" checked />
  Email me about releases
</label>
<label class="label">
  <input type="checkbox" class="toggle" />
  Email me about everything else
</label>
<label class="label">
  <input type="checkbox" class="toggle" disabled />
  Locked off
</label>
<label class="label">
  <input type="checkbox" class="toggle" checked />
  Email me about releases
</label>
<label class="label">
  <input type="checkbox" class="toggle" />
  Email me about everything else
</label>
<label class="label">
  <input type="checkbox" class="toggle" disabled />
  Locked off
</label>
import { Switch } from "@kobalte/core/switch";

<Switch checked={enabled()} onChange={setEnabled}>
  <Switch.Input />
  <Switch.Control class="toggle">
    <Switch.Thumb class="toggle-thumb" />
  </Switch.Control>
  <Switch.Label>Email me about releases</Switch.Label>
</Switch>
<div style="display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 1rem">
  <div class="toggle" data-checked><div class="toggle-thumb" data-checked></div></div>
  <label>Email me about releases</label>
</div>
<div style="display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 0.5rem; margin-bottom: 1rem">
  <div class="toggle"><div class="toggle-thumb"></div></div>
  <label>Email me about everything else</label>
</div>
<div style="display: flex; align-items: center; gap: 0.5rem">
  <div class="toggle" data-disabled><div class="toggle-thumb"></div></div>
  <label>Locked off</label>
</div>

Switch is the one component here whose parts carry no role or attribute of their own: Control, Thumb and the root are all divs with the same data-checked. So the classes go on the parts by hand, and /classless/kobalte only supplies the state.
