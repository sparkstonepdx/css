Kbd

Keyboard keys, filled with the primary color.

ClassDoes what
kbdKey cap: small, bold, primary fill

Basic

<div class="prose">
  <p>Press <kbd class="kbd">Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd class="kbd">K</kbd> to search.</p>
</div>
<div>
  <p>Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> to search.</p>
</div>

Classless: <code>kbd</code> is styled everywhere. With classes, <code>.kbd</code> works on its own, and <code>kbd</code> inside <code>.prose</code> is styled without it.
