Textarea

Same surface as an input, resizable vertically only.

ClassDoes what
textareaBlock-level field, vertical resize
textarea-errorError color, for when aria-invalid is not available

  Notes
  <textarea rows="4" class="textarea" placeholder="Write something"></textarea>
</label>

Composition

Common ways to put this together with other components. The general rules are on Composition.

Message form in a card

Two short fields side by side, a message box, and a send button closing the card.

Same markup in both builds 
<form class="card">
  <div class="flex flip" style="row-gap: 0">
    <label class="label" style="flex: 1"><p>Name</p><input class="input" autocomplete="name" /></label>
    <label class="label" style="flex: 1"><p>Email</p><input class="input" type="email" autocomplete="email" /></label>
  </div>
  <label class="label">
    <p>Message</p>
    <textarea class="textarea" rows="4"></textarea>
    <small>A few sentences is plenty.</small>
  </label>
  <footer class="card-actions flex" style="justify-content: end">
    <button class="btn">Send</button>
  </footer>
</form>
  • Layout: two short fields share a .flex .flip row; row-gap: 0 keeps them from double-spacing when they stack.
  • Surface: .card-actions closes the card with its own rule, which also absorbs the last field's bottom margin.
