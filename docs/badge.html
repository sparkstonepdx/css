Badge

A small pill for counts, tags and statuses.

ClassDoes what
badgeRounded pill on a raised surface
text-errorError-colored text, for validation messages
text-secondaryMuted text

Basic

<div class="prose">
  <p>Status <small class="badge">Draft</small> <small class="badge">3 open</small></p>
</div>
<div>
  <p>Status <small class="chip">Draft</small> <small class="chip">3 open</small></p>
</div>

Muted and error text

<div class="prose">
  <p class="text-secondary">Secondary text</p>
  <small class="text-error">That email address is already taken.</small>
</div>
<div>
  <p class="secondary">Secondary text</p>
  <small class="error">That email address is already taken.</small>
</div>
