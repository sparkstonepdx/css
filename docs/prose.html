Prose

Long-form content: paragraphs, headings, lists, quotes, rules and code. The classless build styles these elements everywhere. The class build scopes them to .prose, so a paragraph inside a component keeps its own spacing.

ClassDoes what
proseStyles p, h1 to h6, ul, ol, li, blockquote, hr, code, pre, samp and kbd inside it
kbdKey cap, usable outside .prose

Headings and paragraphs

<div class="prose">
  <h1>Heading one</h1>
  <h2>Heading two</h2>
  <h3>Heading three</h3>
  <p>Body text sits at 18px with a 1rem bottom margin.</p>

Composition

Common ways to put this together with other components. The general rules are on Composition.

Article page

A long-form page: a heading, a one-line summary under it, body copy with a pull quote, and a closing call to action. One .prose on the article spaces all of it.

Same markup in both builds 
<article class="prose" style="max-width: 80ch">
  <h1>How we rebuilt the booking flow</h1>
  <p class="text-secondary">Three weeks, one form, and half the drop-off.</p>
  <p>The old flow asked for everything up front. Most people left at the second screen.</p>
  <blockquote>We stopped asking for anything we could find out later.</blockquote>
  <h2>What changed</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>One screen instead of four</li>
    <li>Optional fields moved after the booking</li>
  </ul>
  <h2>Have a form like this?</h2>
  <p>A short call is the quickest way to find out what to cut.</p>
  <a href="#" class="btn">Book a call</a>
</article>
  • Text: the summary is .text-secondary, so it reads as a lead-in rather than a second heading.
  • Actions: the button is the only filled thing on the page, at the end, where a reader who finished is ready to act.

Text beside an image

A photo or illustration beside a heading, a paragraph and a link. On a phone the image goes above.

Same markup in both builds 
<section class="flex flip" style="align-items: center">
  <div style="width: 12rem; flex-shrink: 0; aspect-ratio: 1; border-radius: var(--border-radius); background: var(--surface-strong)" role="img" aria-label="Portrait"></div>
  <div class="prose" style="flex: 1">
    <h2>About the studio</h2>
    <p>One person, building and looking after the software small businesses run on.</p>
    <a href="#" class="link">More about the studio</a>
  </div>
</section>
  • Text: .prose sits on the text column, not the row, so the image gets no margins.
  • Layout: the image takes a width and flex-shrink: 0 rather than a flex basis. Under .flip the row becomes a column on a phone, and a basis would turn into a height.

Above a page heading

The trail sits directly above the heading of the page it ends at.

Same markup in both builds 
<nav class="breadcrumbs" aria-label="Breadcrumbs">
  <ol>
    <li><a href="#" class="link">Home</a> <span class="breadcrumbs-separator" aria-hidden="true">/</span></li>
    <li><a href="#" class="link">Catalog</a> <span class="breadcrumbs-separator" aria-hidden="true">/</span></li>
    <li><span aria-current="page">Item name</span></li>
  </ol>
</nav>
<div class="prose">
  <h1>Item name</h1>
  <p class="text-secondary">A line about the item.</p>
</div>
  • Text: the current page is plain text with aria-current; it matches the heading below, so the trail and the page agree.
