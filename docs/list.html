List and row

A list of named things, one per row: a status or picture at the start, a title and a detail, and one action or a › at the end.

alpha The class names and states here are stable. The look is not: these styles can change in a minor release, without a major version bump.

ClassDoes what
listOne raised block holding the rows, split by hairlines
rowA row: an a or button row is tappable as a whole
row-mainThe title, and a muted detail in <small>
row-endOne action, a badge, or a ›
page-headA page's title, a detail, and its actions at the end
page-actionsThe actions in a page head
eyebrowA small label over a heading or section

Basic

Same markup in both builds 
<ul class="list">
  <li><a class="row" href="#"><span class="badge badge-success">Your turn</span><span class="row-main"><span>Battle of the Bag</span><small>Ana, Ben · round 4</small></span><span class="row-end">›</span></a></li>
  <li class="row"><span class="badge">Solo</span><span class="row-main"><span>Solo run</span><small>round 7 · best 1,240</small></span><span class="row-end"><button class="btn btn-secondary btn-sm">Open</button></span></li>
</ul>
