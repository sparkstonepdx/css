Notices take Wordbag's look: as wide as their words (full width with buttons or a detail line), edged in one colour on a 14% tint of it, words at normal weight, the picture at text size. That colour is the new `--notice-tint`, the accent unless an app sets it; corners follow `--radius-box`. Proposal, see DESIGN-REVIEW.md.
