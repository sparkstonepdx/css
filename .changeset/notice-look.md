Notices take Wordbag's look: as wide as their words (full width with buttons or a detail line), edged in the accent on a 14% tint of it, words at normal weight, the picture at text size. Each notice is a re-theme boundary, so an app colours them by setting `--primary` on `.notice`, or overrides from its own stylesheet. Proposal, see DESIGN-REVIEW.md.
